pve-00 · hardware reference

Dell Precision 7820 Tower, system board 0G7W4R (A00). PCIe rows are in the operator-observed physical order, starting at the CPU side; numbers 1–5 here describe that order, not Dell's printed SLOT labels. Connector widths not established per physical position are marked unknown.

Slot planning: what is known

Dell specifies five PCIe Gen 3 x16-length connectors: two wired x16, one x8, one x4 and one x1, plus a separate legacy PCI 32/33 position. The operator confirmed the five PCIe positions and their occupancy in CPU-side order. The P2000 negotiates x16; the GT 710 negotiates x1; the NVMe device negotiates x4 behind Intel VMD. These are device link widths, not a full connector-width map. Before installing an SFP card or another NVMe device in empty position 1 or 5, check that connector's printed SLOT label, electrical wiring, card clearance and CPU dependency. SMBIOS misleadingly reports all SLOT1–SLOT6 as “Available” even with cards installed.
